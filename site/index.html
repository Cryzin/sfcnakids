About

Welcome

Welcome to Sunday School department of Sharon Fellowship Churches of North America. The centralized curriculum and exam for Sunday School were started in the year 2014. There is a team working to ensure that the Sunday school classes are held in a systematic way in all participating churches.

The different pages in this section contain relevant information. For any information or details please feel free to contact SFCNA Sunday School coordinator or committee members.

Our vision

To cultivate and develop a strong spiritual foundation to our children, firmly rooted in God's word by in-depth teaching and sharing of the word of God through life application objectives and activities.

Our mission

To impart the unchanging, incorruptible word of God to the future generation to equip them to face the day-to-day challenges of the modern world.

Class and age

Classes follow school grade

The age for the class will be based on Grade of the student. Individual churches should set classes according to school grade. It will be based on the grade of the student on December 1st.

KGPrimary (Grades 1-2)Middler (Grades 3-4)Pre-teen (Grades 5-6)Young Teen (Grades 7-8)High School (Grades 9-12)

These are the only classes recognized by SFCNA at this time for conducting the centralized examination. Churches are free to have classes other than these. SFCNA will not have a centralized examination for any class other than these 6 classes. If more classes are needed, feel free to make suggestions to SFCNA Sunday School committee.

Who does what

Roles and responsibilities

Role of the Sunday School Committee
  • Provide all necessary instructions and guidelines in a timely manner.
  • Organize final examination, evaluation of answer papers, and declaration of results. Winners will be announced at the following SFCNA Conference.
  • Deliver Sunday School textbook which is not available within the USA, since additional materials are required for High school students (ages between 16-18), kindly make sure that the local church headmaster takes responsibility to inform the SFCNA Sunday School committee the need of materials at least by the second week of November.
  • Please feel free to contact SFCNA Sunday School committee for any further assistance or information.
Role of the Local Church
  • Setting up different classes based on the age group, in line with the school currently attended by the student (refer class division). Allocation of Sunday School teachers.
  • Ordering materials, both for students and teachers, as necessary.
  • Contact SFCNA Sunday School Committee for High School books (Sharon Fellowship Church Sunday School Text Book).
  • Updating SFCNA Sunday School Committee the details of classes, the number of students in each class, list of teachers.
  • Conducting centralized annual examination as directed by Sunday School Committee of SFCNA.
