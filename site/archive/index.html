Curriculum changes

Scripture Press and Radiant Life, 2021

Curriculum update, June 2021

Starting from Fall 2021, all the participating churches are recommended to move to Scripture Press Curriculum. The centralized exam will be from Scripture Press Curriculum. Please note the following changes:

  • The questions and activity suggestions for KG, Primary and Middler classes will be uploaded on this website. The centralized exam will be from those questions.
  • Class changes in 2021 should be done on December. From 2022, churches should plan to change classes in August to align with School year and Scripture press curriculum.
  • The new classes should be: KG (KG), Primary (Grades 1-2), Middler (Grades 3-4), Junior (Grades 5-6), Young Teen (Grades 7-8), High School (Grades 9-12).
  • Centralized exam for 2021 will be held in December. From 2022, the centralized exam will be held in August/ September.
  • Sunday School year will be from September to August.

Other instructions
  • Each church is responsible for ordering the books from Radiant Life publications. It is highly recommended to order materials whenever it is available and not wait till the need appears. In the past, books have gone out of stock and churches have faced shortages.
  • The website to order Radiant Life curriculum is: gospelpublishing.com, Radiant Life.
  • It is recommended that each church follows the dates in the curriculum so that none of the sessions are missed.
  • The final exam will be from any one session but SFCNA recommends that all 4 sessions be completed. If needed, the teachers might be required to take 2 lessons in a single day to ensure that all lessons are completed.
  • The sessions based on Radiant Life Curriculum are: Winter Curriculum (December, January, February), Spring Curriculum (March, April, May), Summer Curriculum (June, July, August) and Fall Curriculum (September, October, November).
  • Full verses should be taught in class and not partial verse. Radiant Life has some memory verses which are not complete based on the Bible. For centralized examination, we will not be allowing incomplete verses. All teachers are advised to teach full verses.
