Guidance for teachers

  • The class teacher should conduct a test and quizzes at frequent intervals.
  • Students will not be given books. It is crucial to provide lesson notes and questions to students.
  • Review questions are at the end of each lesson.
  • While reviewing and teaching, please note grammatical mistakes, out-of-context examples, phrases, etc. Provide that as feedback to the curriculum revision team.
  • If there are suggestions to improve a lesson, please provide them to the curriculum revision team.
  • As we adopt this new curriculum, please be patient as we improve it to best meet the needs of our students.
  • The books will be reused. Please keep them in good condition and return them after the lessons are over.
